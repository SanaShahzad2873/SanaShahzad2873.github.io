About Sana Shahzad's Delights

Our Story

Founded in 2015 by Chef Sana Shahzad, our restaurant began as a small passion project with just six tables. What started as a dream to share authentic global flavors has blossomed into one of the city's most celebrated dining destinations.

Our Philosophy

"Every dish tells a story" - this belief guides our kitchen. We combine traditional cooking techniques with modern presentations, using only the freshest locally-sourced ingredients to create memorable culinary experiences.
